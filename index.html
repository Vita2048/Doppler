Source: 440 Hz. Microphone: 440 Hz.

At rest, equally spaced crests arrive at the same frequency they leave the source.

The source approaches. Its new wavefronts travel toward the mic.

Sound takes time to arrive. The amber ray shows where the sound now reaching the mic was emitted.

Closer crests → shorter arrival period → higher frequency.

The peak lies to the right of the rest marker. The source still emits 440 Hz.

Wider-spaced crests → longer arrival period → lower frequency.

The peak falls to the left of the rest marker as the arriving sound becomes lower in pitch.
