This video explains the Doppler effect — why a passing sound changes pitch.

At rest, the microphone hears the source’s own frequency.

As the source approaches, crests crowd together, so the microphone hears a higher pitch.

After passing, crests stretch apart, and the pitch drops.

That shift is the Doppler effect. The source keeps the same note; motion changes how often crests arrive.
